# J5a — Food moments and the ordering funnel — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The food funnel feels like a meal being made: the kitchen's yes lands as a moment, a dish flies into a living cart bar, the item sheet leads with its picture and says how many it feeds, family orders have a table, checkout puts how you pay first, a closed kitchen takes a pre-order, someone else can receive (and track) your order, reorder is two taps, and a rejection explains itself.

**Architecture:** Every figure is the server's: popularity counts (`catalog.menu`), portions and dish labels (merchant-set catalog columns), the points estimate (`orders.quote`), opening hours for pre-order slots (`catalog.menu` card), and the alternative kitchens with their carried-over totals (`orders.alternatives`). The customer app keeps pure, unit-tested helpers next to its screens (`features/food/*.ts`, `features/orders/*.ts`) and the screens only display. Motion uses Reanimated transforms/opacity only, one overlay node for the flight, and every animation has a reduced-motion variant.

**Tech Stack:** Zod + tRPC contracts (`packages/contracts`), NestJS API (`apps/api`), Prisma (`packages/db`), Expo customer app (`apps/customer`), Merchant app editor (`apps/merchant`), `@driver/ui`, vitest.

Spec: `docs/specs/2026-10-05-customer-joy.md` §5.5 (J5a). Findings and signature ideas: `docs/research/ui-ux-audit/2026-10-05-joy/2-food-funnel.md` (F-03, F-06–F-14, F-18, F-21–F-25, S-1, S-2, S-5). Art: `docs/specs/2026-10-06-sketchbook-style.md`.

**Money rules:** none change. o7 only *reports* the earn rule the ledger already applies at close (`orderPoints` + `allocatePoints`, 1 point per 100 دينار of platform revenue, cap 50, organiser bonus 10 %). o12 "who pays" maps onto the two payment methods that already exist (cash collected at the door from whoever receives it; wallet charged to the orderer); no new payer, no split. Points for «للسفرة» lines go to the orderer — the existing rule for untagged lines.

**Before you start:** other sessions work in this repo (tips/driver photos, saved places, home/search, الرجعة/wallet, the Expo upgrade). Stay in the food funnel; stage only the files a task names; commit after every item; never push.
