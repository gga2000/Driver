# Seasons and Ramadan mode (J6) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Grow the J1a quiet days into the season system: ops set typed periods (quiet, Ramadan, Eid, a special Friday) in the Console; `system.season` tells every app what today allows plus, in Ramadan, the iftar and suhoor times for both timetables; the customer app shows a calm season card on home, lets the person pick their timetable once, and offers an «على الفطور» slot at checkout; no offers are pushed in the 20 minutes before iftar; kitchens can be capped per scheduled slot (off by default).

**Architecture:** The `quiet_periods` table gains a `kind`, the switches, an optional home-card line, a per-period Shia maghrib offset and per-day iftar overrides (additive columns, quiet rows keep their meaning). Two pure modules in `apps/api/src/modules/controls/` do the thinking: `prayer-times.ts` (solar maghrib and fajr, the two timetables, overrides) and `season.ts` (compose today's `PublicSeason` from the live rows, the promo hold for the notify engine, the validation of a new period). `ControlsService` only loads rows, caches and audits. The customer app keeps its season store; a new `features/season` folder holds the pure Ramadan helpers, the timetable preference and the `SeasonCard`.

**Tech Stack:** Zod + tRPC contracts, NestJS API, Prisma, Expo customer app, Next.js Console, vitest.

## Decisions locked in this plan

1. **One table.** Periods stay in `quiet_periods` (renaming would break J1a readers mid-flight). `kind` defaults to `quiet`, so every existing row and `setQuietDays` call means exactly what it meant.
2. **Kinds and switches.** `quiet` forces celebrations, sounds, promos and accent off and has no home card (as today). `ramadan`, `eid`, `friday_special` carry their own switches (defaults: all on) and an optional home-card line (`home_card_ar`; Ramadan and Eid have default copy in i18n, a special Friday shows a card only with ops text).
3. **Overlaps.** A quiet day inside Ramadan (19–21 Ramadan) wins every switch (`kind: 'quiet'`, no accent, no Eid card), but the Ramadan block (iftar and suhoor) stays: it is service, not celebration, and the Ramadan card stays, calm. Two periods of the same non-quiet kind may not overlap (`season_overlap`).
4. **Never assume a start day.** The panel sets a Ramadan or Eid period over the union of the days (earliest start to latest end). Copy never names the day («رمضان كريم», «عيدكم مبارك»), never «اليوم أول رمضان».
5. **Two timetables, picked once on the device.** `sunni` («توقيت أهل السنة») and `shia` («توقيت الشيعة»). The server returns both; the app shows the picked one, and until a pick the home card asks for it (it never shows one as "the" time). The pick lives on the device (like the tracking-sounds switch); `system.season({ timetable })` echoes it in `ramadan.iftarAt/suhoorUntil` for clients that want one answer.
6. **The maths.** Standard low-precision solar position (the praytimes.org / USNO almanac formulas: mean anomaly, ecliptic longitude, declination, equation of time), hour angle for the sun's centre at −0.833° (refraction + semidiameter) for sunset and at −18° for fajr, three fixed-point iterations at the event time. Aziziyah 32.91 N, 45.06 E, UTC+3 all year. Maghrib (iftar) = sunset + timetable offset, **rounded up** to the minute (never early); fajr (suhoor ends) **rounded down** (never late). Offsets: Sunni 0 min, Shia `SHIA_MAGHRIB_OFFSET_MIN = 15` (named, period-overridable, **verify locally**). Fajr angle 18° for both (the earlier, safer fajr; **verify locally**). Ops can override any day's time per timetable in the Console.
7. **The iftar slot.** Server value `slotAt = iftarAt − IFTAR_SLOT_LEAD_MIN (10)`: the food arrives before the adhan. Checkout offers it (when ≥ 45 min away, the existing schedule lead) as «على الفطور · {adhan time}» with a note. It is an ordinary `scheduledFor`; nothing else changes in placement.
8. **Slot caps.** The merchant side has no capacity setting, so `ORDERS_RULES.slotCap = { perSlot: null, byMerchant: {} , windowMin: 30 }` (default off): when a cap applies, a scheduled order is refused with `slot_full` if the kitchen already has that many live scheduled orders within ±15 min.
9. **Promo hold.** `ControlsService.promoHold(at)` → `{ reason: 'quiet_day' }` (suppress), `{ reason: 'season' }` (a season with promos off: suppress), `{ reason: 'iftar', until }` (from the earliest timetable's iftar − 20 min to the latest timetable's iftar: defer to `until`). The notify engine asks before every `marketing` delivery, at dispatch and at send.
10. **Skipped (flagged):** courier iftar-break copy on tracking ETAs, kitchen batching, Friday-prayer hold (needs prayer times for dhuhr; the season system can add it the same way).

### Task 3: `prayer-times.ts` (TDD)

Tests first (`prayer-times.test.ts`):
- Baghdad 2027-02-08 sunset within 1 min of 17:40 local and sunrise of 06:53 (published almanac, r-astro / solarwatch).
- Aziziyah 2027-02-08: Sunni iftar 17:39, Shia 17:54, suhoor (fajr 18°) 05:26 — the numbers this plan records; any change to the maths shows here.
- Maghrib is rounded up, fajr down (a sunset at hh:mm:01 → hh:mm+1).
- Overrides replace one timetable's time on one day only.
- A period's Shia offset replaces the default.
