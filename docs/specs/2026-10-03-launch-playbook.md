# Driver (درايفر) — Launch Playbook

Date: 2026-10-03 · Status: approved — Plan 10 complete.

## 1. Launch shape
Big-bang public launch of every vertical built by launch day (Food, city taxi/tuktuk, الرجعة), preceded by a three-day private dress rehearsal with staff and drivers (real orders, cash collection, settlement). Khat enrolment, parcels and grocery follow as their sub-projects ship.

## 2. Day-1 supply
10 restaurants (incl. مطعم خالد، مشويات الحاج كريم، مأكولات الشام، مطعم المسافر); 10 bike couriers + 15 tuktuks on food; 15 taxis; 6 intercity drivers announcing on the garage boards. Each restaurant visited the day before: printer paired, menu checked, busy mode explained, WhatsApp group with the dispatcher.

## 3. Controls
Capacity throttle per zone (max concurrent orders) with honest ETA messaging; kill switches per vertical, zone, restaurant; a status banner to every open app. Shift guarantee active. Dispatchers on shift at every peak; Ali on the dispatch board at both peaks in week one.

## 4. Rota
Ali + 2 dispatcher/support + 1 field ops; two shifts 10:00–24:00. Daily 09:00 15-minute review of yesterday's numbers and disputes; evening metrics check; every complaint answered same day; cash collection round at 23:00 in week one; Gold-badge push for the top 10 drivers after week one.

## 5. Marketing
Launch video via the perfume-ad engine workflow; referral push (2,000 points both sides); free delivery on first 3 orders; restaurant-of-the-day posts; الرجعة message aimed at Aziziyah students in Baghdad through university groups; driver recruitment post showing real earnings; WhatsApp groups per vehicle class and per restaurant.

## 6. Week-one metrics on the wall
Median delivery < 35 min · acceptance > 85% · zero disputes unresolved > 24 h · 30+ orders/day by day 7 · 20+ الرجعة seats booked · ledger balanced every night.

- [ ] Company registered; Play organization account; Apple enrollment attempted
- [ ] WhatsApp Business verified, templates approved
- [ ] SMS gateway live; push working on 5 test phones
- [ ] 34 zones verified by drivers; 50+ landmarks and meeting points seeded with photos
- [ ] Price tables entered with real Aziziyah fares
- [ ] Simulator run clean; human review of ledger, identity, safety modules
- [ ] Dress rehearsal completed; all drivers checked in with selfie
- [ ] Support canned responses loaded; AI first line tested in Iraqi Arabic
- [ ] Kill switches tested
