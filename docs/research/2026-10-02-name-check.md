# Name check: "Driver" / "درايفر" / "Drayver" — 2026-10-02

Scope: delivery + ride-hailing super-app for Aziziyah / Wasit, Iraq. Checked via web search and page fetches only. No registrations attempted.

**Overall risk: HIGH** — not because one company owns the name, but because "Driver" / "درايفر" is a generic, descriptive word that is already used in dozens of app titles (every ride-hailing platform ships a "<Brand> Driver" / "<Brand> درايفر" app for its captains), so the name is unlikely to be protectable as a trademark in class 39/9/42 and will be nearly impossible to rank or search for in the stores. "Drayver" is cleaner but still crowded by near-homophones (Dryver, Draiver, DRYVR, DRYVRS, DRVN, Drivo, DRV).

## 2. Iraqi / GCC companies already using درايفر

- **SAIQ (Kuwait)** markets its captain app as "درايفر" / "DRV - Driver Captain" — a real GCC transport operator using the exact Arabic word as a product name. This is the most direct regional precedent.
- Talabista (delivery) and "فور درايفر" also brand their driver apps as "درايفر".
- No Iraqi company was found that uses "درايفر" as its *consumer-facing* brand. Iraqi players (Baly, Toters, Tabat, Talabaty, Careem, Bolt/Taxify, Amin, Hader, Gubal, Dijlah, Jacksi) all use "Driver"/"Captain" only as the suffix of their driver-side app.
- A freelance listing on Khamsat titled ["تصميم UI/UX احترافي لتطبيق درايفر Driver App"](https://khamsat.com/designing/app-design/4375601-...) shows the term is treated as a generic product category in the Arabic freelance market, not as a brand.

## 4. Trademark conflicts

**What could be verified**
- Iraq trademark office: Registrar of Trademarks, Ministry of Industry and Minerals (Baghdad), plus a separate KRG registry in Erbil ([Al Tamimi overview](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update/september-2/articles/overview-of-the-trademarks-law-in-iraq/), [Global Law Experts](https://globallawexperts.com/trademark-services-in-iraq/)). Filing is via the ministry's Unified Registration e-portal; an unopposed application takes roughly 6–12 months; protection is 10 years renewable.
- Iraq is a Paris Convention / WIPO member but **not** a Madrid Protocol member as of the sources found (Saudi, UAE, Qatar joined recently; Iraq is not listed). Any Iraqi mark must be filed nationally.
- Iraqi law allows marks that acquire distinctiveness through use, which implies a purely descriptive word like "Driver" for transport services will face a descriptiveness objection on first filing.
- US records (proxy for how examiners treat the word): "DRIVER" (Messina Technology, class 35/42, software) [pending](https://trademarks.justia.com/983/64/driver-98364292.html); "DRYVER" and "DRIVR" are registered US marks for driver services. These do not reach Iraq but show the word and its variants are heavily claimed in the transport/software space.

**What could NOT be verified**
- WIPO Global Brand Database (branddb.wipo.int) is a JavaScript application and could not be queried from this environment; no class 39 / 9 / 42 result list for "DRIVER" or "DRAYVER" was obtained.
- The Iraqi Ministry of Industry portal has no public, indexable search; a pre-filing search must be ordered through a local agent (quoted at 1–2 weeks by agents).
- The KRG registry was not checked.
- GCC national registries (Saudi SAIP, UAE MoE, Kuwait) were not queried; SAIQ's "درايفر" product name may or may not be registered in Kuwait.

## Conclusion

- **"Driver" / "درايفر"**: HIGH risk. Generic term, used as a product name by SAIQ (Kuwait) and as a suffix by essentially every competitor in Iraq. Weak trademark position, near-zero store searchability, and user confusion with the "Baly Driver / Toters Driver" style captain apps.
- **"Drayver"**: MEDIUM risk. No exact match found, but Dryver, Draiver, DRYVR, DRYVRS and DRIVR occupy the same sound; a Latin-script mark may still be registrable in Iraq since none of those operate there, but it offers little defensibility and is hard to spell from speech in Arabic.

### Recommended alternatives (if keeping the concept)

1. **درايفر + distinctive suffix**: "Drayver Go" / "درايفر گو", or "Drayver IQ" (avoid: collides with Driver iQ US SaaS). Better: **"Drayver Wasit"** or **"درايفرك"** (Drayverak, "your driver") — adds a coined element that can be registered.
2. **Coined spelling with Arabic-first identity**: **"Drayfr"** / **"دريفر"** (shorter, uncommon transliteration), or **"Dravo"** / **"درافو"** — pronounceable in Iraqi dialect, no store or trademark matches found in this pass, and keeps the "driver" cue without the generic word.

Either option should still get a paid pre-filing search at the Baghdad registry before committing to branding spend.
